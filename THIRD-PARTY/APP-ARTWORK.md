# Asset provenance

Sun logo generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompt: “Create one premium app logo asset for a smart TV launcher named Sun. Transparent background, square composition, isolated symbol only, no text. A recognizable radiant sun: luminous warm amber circular orb with eight short softly tapered rounded rays integrated close to the orb, not thin sticks, subtle polished glass depth, warm gold to coral optical edges, restrained luxury Apple-inspired material clarity but original brand identity. Frontal view, symmetrical, simple enough legible at 40 pixels. Centered with generous transparent margin, no badge, no backdrop, no letters, no watermark. Intended as a real UI logo, crisp clean silhouette and alpha.”

Original PNG retained; optimized WebP is used in the interface.

Netflix, YouTube, Apple TV and Prime Video vector marks sourced from Simple Icons (current package and v11.15.0 for Prime Video), retaining recognizable silhouettes and brand colors. Brand marks remain the property of their owners; use here identifies the linked apps. https://simpleicons.org/

Casting icons and star field are original SVG artwork. Existing 4K paper backgrounds are original procedural raster artwork.

Visual direction references Apple's published materials guidance; this is an original web implementation, not Apple's native Liquid Glass framework or an Apple product. https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials

JioHotstar artwork is the official JioStar media-toolkit PNG: https://cdn.jiostar.com/jiostar/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/JioHot-Star-1.png (source page: https://www.jiostar.com/mediatoolkit/all-logos/). It identifies the app; it is not newly generated artwork.

Chrome shortcut: original simplified SVG rendering of the Chrome mark, matching the existing glass tile system. Google Chrome and its mark belong to Google. No Chrome binary is bundled.

Revision 0.11 adds Claude, Gemini, Steam, Epic Games and Google Docs vectors from the Simple Icons develop branch (retrieved 2026-10-21); the OpenAI mark is from Simple Icons 11.15.0. Simple Icons CC0 license is retained in SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md; trademarks remain with their owners. Google and Chrome original vectors come from Devicon, with the existing Devicon MIT license retained. Glass depth is CSS, not an AI alteration of these marks.

Antigravity: official product favicon, https://antigravity.google/favicon.svg, retrieved 2026-10-21. Google trademark; used unchanged to identify its app. Glass treatment applies to the surrounding tile, not the mark.

Prime Video: original 144px touch icon from https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/digital/video/DVUI/favicons/apple-touch-icon-144x144.png linked by primevideo.com; Amazon trademark. JioHotstar: original icon from https://secure-media.hotstar.com/web-assets/prod/jv-jhs_favicon.ico linked by hotstar.com, losslessly converted to PNG for the asset server. Retrieved 2026-10-21. Marks unchanged; no generated substitutes.
